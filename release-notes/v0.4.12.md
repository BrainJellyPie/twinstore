TwinStore v0.4.12 includes the fixes integrated since v0.4.11, with particular
attention to failed setup steps, customer evidence and simulation accuracy.

- **Automatic starter products:** recover incomplete model assignments instead
  of stopping store setup when a rack is omitted or assigned more than once.
- **Customer evidence:** preserve CSV identifiers, encodings and local timestamps;
  reject malformed tables and incomplete Excel formula results before generation.
  Retrying customer generation keeps the original attachment requirement.
- **Simulation accuracy:** preserve physical shelf placement, actual offer prices,
  reachable purchase/checkout routes and complete shopper exits. Respect stock,
  budgets, staffing limits and customer-generation concurrency.
- **Comparable studies:** freeze accepted product artwork across experiment arms,
  keep consistent successful customer cohorts and handle undefined ranking metrics
  explicitly. Preserve intended price levels in generated conjoint studies.
- **Reliable saves and recovery:** clean up failed job starts and partial uploads,
  protect existing history, and prevent late work from recreating deleted stores.
  Status-save failures no longer leave recoverable workers pending indefinitely.
- **Results and playback:** completed empty runs can finish playback, and movement
  maps support the largest accepted store grids without a call-stack overflow.

All published branch heads were checked against main before this release.
The build workflow verifies the packaged launchers on Linux x64, Windows and macOS;
the Linux arm64 archive is cross-built.
