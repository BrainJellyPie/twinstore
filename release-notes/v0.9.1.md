TwinStore v0.9.1 is a fixes release: receipt photos read more reliably with
small models, numbers follow the selected language, and keyboard and
screen-reader users keep their place.

- **Receipt reading.** Models count product rows before transcribing them,
  keep signed quantities and amounts so refunds are caught in review, read the
  printed row behind an inferred total, and are retried when they copy the
  JSON Schema instead of the receipt. Shelf capture recognizes more currency
  marks (₹, R$, Vietnamese dong) and keeps reference numbers, purchase limits
  and reversed multipack sizes from becoming selling prices.
- **Numbers in your language.** Percentages, distances and limits are written
  the way the selected language writes them ("38 %", "1 273 m" in French), and
  Spanish and French price fields accept a decimal comma.
- **Keyboard and screen readers.** Focus moves to the right place after opening
  a shopper, the group interview or a tracked shopper; disabled buttons say why
  they are off; test results and errors are announced; and choices that showed
  the picked one only by colour now tell screen readers too. In the floor
  editor, a fixture can be dragged to another cell.
- **Fewer surprises.** Product bulk editing and automatic starter filling ask
  before discarding changes, opening a page while the backend restarts no
  longer lands on the browser's error page and loses unsaved work, names cut
  short show in full on hover, and an
  address with an over-long ID is refused as invalid instead of causing a
  server error.

Small models can still misread meaning even when their JSON is valid. Review
generated customers, receipt rows and photo prices before relying on them.
