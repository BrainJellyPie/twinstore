TwinStore v0.7.0 connects the two twins through the same customers, turns the
online analyst's answers into tests, screens every quick change in seconds, and
gives the whole app a pass for legibility, phones and lost links.

- **The same customers in the store and online.** An online run whose shoppers
  are the store's own customers now reads each of them next to the newest store
  run of the same people: who bought in both, only in the store, only online or
  nowhere, what they spent in each channel, and how that splits by age and
  gender. The run analyst reads the same lines, so "who prefers the web shop?"
  has an answer.
- **From an answer to a test.** Every report line an analyst answer cites links
  to the chart that draws it, which scrolls into view and lights up. When an
  answer points at something a quick experiment can change (a popup, fees, a
  forced sign-in, empty searches, slow pages, delivery costs, abandoned carts,
  prices), **Test it** chips open the run form with that change already ticked.
- **Screen every quick change at once.** **Scan these changes** sends 150 rule
  shoppers through the store and a copy for each applicable quick experiment in
  about ten seconds, marks each one clearly better, clearly worse or unclear,
  and **Tick the clear winners** sets up the proper comparison. While you design,
  a quick read shows the draft's difference from the saved store before you
  test it.
- **Easier to find things.** Online variants sit under the store they were
  copied from, a find box filters stores and runs, the address keeps the open
  store and run so a reload or a shared link comes back to them, and the open
  entry scrolls into view. The report's section strip follows your reading.
  The home page opens with **Continue where you left off**, and the store tabs
  work from the home and settings pages too.
- **Readable everywhere.** No label is smaller than about 11 px any more. The
  top bar keeps the store's name readable from phone to wide screen (at 1024 px
  the tabs used to run over the logo). On a phone the online report, the
  marketing studio's tabs, the A/B notice, shelf capture's drop zone and the
  area description all fit.
- **Old links explain themselves.** A deleted store, a deleted online run or an
  address that matches no page shows what happened and the way back, instead of
  a bare "Something went wrong!", an empty page or a server message.
- **Fixes from the shared work.** Browsing customers keep their budget in online
  decisions and interviews, free purchases and repeat visits count in the
  store/online comparison, quick scans use the run's arrival mix, 100 % discounts
  stay free offers, analyst context is bounded by bytes for long conversations,
  and up to 500 price changes come back from an online variant to a store
  layout.
