TwinStore v0.8.1 turns an online price change into an in-store A/B test and
fixes a few slow or awkward screens.

- **Test an online price change in the store.** In the online report, a
  variant's **Keep it in the store** block now has **Test it in the store**: the
  marketing studio's A/B builder opens with the variant's price changes on the
  chosen layout, ready to run on the same store shoppers. A variant with more
  than the 20 changes an A/B test holds says so and points to keeping it as a
  layout instead.
- **Results timeline drawn sooner.** After switching layouts the timeline's bars
  appear once the run is loaded instead of waiting until the 3D store is built.
- **Smaller fixes.** The Compare runs table's metric column blends into the
  dialog on wide screens, and the new-store format list shows about four rows
  at a time.
- **Structured local-model answers.** Online shopper choices that small JSON-mode
  models wrap in `properties` are read as the choices they are, with required
  fields still validated and copied schemas not treated as clicks.
