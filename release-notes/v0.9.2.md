TwinStore v0.9.2 fixes store creation with Claude Haiku 5.5.

- **Stores can be created with Claude Haiku 5.5 again.** On v0.9.1 most new
  stores stopped at "Customer generation failed … the model's reply was not in
  the expected form." Each generated customer must be of the type its request
  asks for, and Claude, asked to answer without thinking first, wrote the ten
  customers of a request in another order. Customer generation now leaves
  Claude's thinking on: in real runs with Claude Haiku 5.5, 30 of 30 requests
  wrote their customers, against 3 of 12 before.
- Shopper decisions and the other short answers still answer directly, so runs
  are as fast as before. A customer-writing request takes about twice the
  output tokens it did; the estimate shown before writing includes them.

Stores whose customer generation failed on v0.9.1 can be finished from the
store's preparation screen with **Try again**.
