TwinStore v0.5.0 adds **online stores**: browser-style web shops that virtual
shoppers browse page by page, alongside the physical-store twin.

- **42 ready-made web shops** — groceries, quick commerce, convenience,
  pharmacy, beauty, grooming, pets, baby, toys, hobbies, art and craft,
  stationery, books, electronics, kitchen, home, garden, florist, fashion,
  jewellery, sports, outdoor, cycling, travel, music, coffee, gourmet, baking,
  plant-based, snacks, drinks, liquor, food delivery, café ordering, hardware,
  party, a marketplace, flash sales and senior care — about 2,500 listings
  drawn from the bundled catalog, most with photos. A store's own layout can
  also go online.
- **Shoppers that use a browser.** Each page is drawn as a screenshot with a
  numbered badge on every clickable element. A vision model picks one option
  (search and what to type, back, open product 3, open the menu or settings,
  add a payment method, close the tab…); a text model reads the page outline
  instead; rule shoppers run instantly. Shoppers have missions — buy, window-
  shop, compare, hunt bargains, find a gift, reorder — plus a device, budget,
  patience, web comfort and eyesight.
- **Design options to test:** layout and speed, search quality, sponsored
  listings, urgency and social proof, welcome popups, cookie banners, delivery
  fees and thresholds, coupons, guest checkout, checkout steps, payment
  methods, last-step service fees, pre-ticked add-ons and return policies.
  Save any change as a variant and compare up to four on the same shoppers.
- **Research reports:** paired differences with bootstrap 95 % intervals,
  funnel and exit pages, a friction and dark-pattern audit, exit surveys
  (satisfaction, ease, NPS, return intent, biggest annoyance), searches that
  found nothing, product performance, click maps, segments by mission, device,
  age, gender and eyesight, page-by-page replays and a clickstream CSV.
- **Accessibility:** low vision, red–green colour blindness and older eyes,
  as a realistic mix or for everyone; vision shoppers see filtered screenshots
  and you can preview any page through those eyes.
- **Dependable runs:** cart quantities stay within stock, only stores in the
  same currency can be compared, a model answer cut off by its length limit is
  retried once with more room, smart search also matches categories and tags,
  wishlists and large carts page like other lists, screenshots grow to fit an
  open menu or dialog, a variant's edited products and design are saved
  together, saving is blocked while any amount is invalid, the live preview
  keeps its numbers and device when a request fails, a store's own layout
  imports each product once, and a project cannot be deleted while one of its
  online runs is active.
- **Local model fixes since v0.4.14:** native Ollama Qwen3.5 requests keep
  short answer budgets, and blank generated customer narratives are rejected
  before they reach a population.

The online-store interface is available in all six languages. Rule shoppers
follow illustrative, uncalibrated probabilities; use model shoppers and read
differences between variants rather than absolute conversion rates.
