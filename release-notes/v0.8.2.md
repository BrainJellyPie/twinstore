TwinStore v0.8.2 improves local-model setup, automatic products and shelf
capture, and brings the latest interface fixes into the main release.

- **Automatic products with small models.** Compact catalog prompts leave
  more room for complete answers. Oversized product tasks recover in bounded
  groups before falling back to individual categories. Clearer selection
  instructions reduce copied candidate lists while SKU, exclusion, currency
  and shelf-capacity checks remain enforced. Actual 2B CPU runs covered a
  198-rack hypermarket, a café and a bookstore through final placement.
- **More dependable model data.** Numbered profile and hourly-traffic fields
  constrain output shape; output-mode-specific caches keep JSON and JSON
  Schema decisions separate. Model startup immediately shows preparation.
  Package matching searches observed candidates before applying its limit
  and handles printed brand/size order without mixing quantity units.
- **Shelf-photo corrections.** Recognized savings and coupon amounts no
  longer become exact selling prices. Actual label values wrapped in
  `properties` are preserved instead of silently turning into empty labels.
  Unsaved corrections survive a look at Shelf Lab and trigger a confirmation
  before leaving. Product fields have accessible names.
- **Simulation and experiment accuracy.** Imported A/B prices retain their
  currency. Whole-shop browsers no longer have a hidden category target, and
  online reports distinguish unanswered exit surveys from usable answers.
- **Layout and interface fixes.** Delete layouts while retaining at least one,
  reopen the last viewed floor, see specific layout errors, and use arrow keys
  on studio/store tabs. Dialog focus, narrow-screen reports, shopper labels,
  map dragging and model-readiness notices are improved.

Small models can still misread meaning even when their JSON is valid. Review
generated customer budgets and decisions, and photo prices: a Korean discount
probe still omitted the discount word from its transcription. This release
does not claim to resolve those semantic limitations.
